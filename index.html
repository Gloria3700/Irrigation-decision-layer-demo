REAL PUBLIC CONTEXT

Why collective irrigation is a strong pilot for the RP

The Authion basin already has collective allocation, annual plans, many withdrawal points, changing crop needs and regulatory drought constraints. A3P explicitly aims to combine crop-water need modelling with AI-based hydrological availability forecasts to support dynamic irrigation management.

~600irrigating farms in the Authion basin
16–18k hairrigated annually
23–30 Mm³withdrawn in a typical year range reported publicly
~2,200surface- and groundwater withdrawal points
10management units under the Authion OUGC framework
A3Pcrop demand + hydrology + AI + satellite information
What is already operational?

Collective allocation & monitoring

Public descriptions of OUGC Authion show annual water-volume requests, individual and collective review, a Plan Annuel de Répartition (PAR), and possible collective reductions when requests exceed available volume for a management unit/resource.

  • Spatial inventory of users, withdrawal points, crops and resources
  • Annual allocation planning and regulatory documentation
  • Withdrawal-volume monitoring and drought restrictions
  • Potential tours d’eau, notifications and dynamic dashboards
What is the research gap?

From monitoring to forecast-informed action

A3P describes a digital decision-support service that adjusts collective irrigation strategies using both actual crop needs and water availability. The RP adds the explicit decision layer: which predictive information matters, in which state, under which strategy, and with what action?

One analytical chain, four work packages

WP A

Decision context

Reconstruct current practice: actor, state, actions, objectives, constraints, horizon and baseline rule.

→
WP B

Information value

Hold the state fixed, vary forecast information, and test what changes action, timing, intensity or outcome.

→
WP C

Strategy value

With the same state and forecast, compare current practice, simple rules, risk-aware and robust formulations.

→
WP D

Decision service

Return a traceable recommendation, reason and review horizon that can be integrated into a digital service.
